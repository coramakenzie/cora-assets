One chair
one artist
all of her time

At a lot of salons, your stylist starts on someone else while your color is processing. That’s why highlights can take three or four hours.

Deanna books one client at a time and stays with you from start to finish. Most appointments take about an hour and a half. She’s been doing hair for 29 years.
