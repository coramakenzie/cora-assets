Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Bananas Hair respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you text or call to book, we receive your name, phone number, the messages you send, and any photos of your hair you choose to share. During appointments we may keep notes about your services, color formulas, allergies or sensitivities you tell us about, and your appointment history.

Information from this website. This site does not have accounts, contact forms or online payments, and it does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The quiz on the site runs in your browser and your answers are not sent to us.

Information collected automatically by service providers. Like most websites, the company that hosts this site may keep basic server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visited, to keep the site running and secure. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers and Google receives your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, reschedule and remind you about appointments.
  • To provide your services safely, including remembering your color formulas and any allergies.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To keep basic business and tax records as required by law.

How we share your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their marketing. We only share information:

  • With service providers that help us run the business, such as our phone carrier, website host or payment processor, only as needed to provide their service.
  • If required by law, or to protect the safety of our clients, ourselves or others.

Photos

We only post photos of clients’ hair with permission. If you would like a photo of you removed from this website or our social media, text (602) 803-3496 and we will remove it.

How long we keep information

We keep client notes for as long as you are a client and for a reasonable time afterward, in case you return. We keep business records for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your client notes at any time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them online. Appointments for children are booked by a parent or guardian.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we have about you.
  • You can ask us to stop texting you at any time. See our Text Messaging Terms.
  • You can block or clear cookies and stored data in your browser settings.

To make a request, text or call (602) 803-3496.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
