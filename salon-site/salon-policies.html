Salon Policies

Last updated November 16, 2026

These policies keep the schedule fair for everyone. By booking an appointment with Bananas Hair, you agree to them.

Booking

Appointments are booked by text or phone at (602) 803-3496. Your appointment is confirmed only once Deanna replies with a date and time.

Please tell Deanna what service you want when you book. If you are a new client or want a big color change, send a current photo of your hair so she can plan the right amount of time.

Pricing

Prices depend on the service, your hair length and thickness, and the products used. Deanna will tell you the expected price before your service starts. If something changes during your appointment that affects the price, she will tell you before doing the extra work.

Appointment times listed on this website, such as “about 90 minutes” for color and highlights, are typical estimates and are not guaranteed. Your appointment may take more or less time depending on your hair and the service.

Cancellations and rescheduling

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Because Deanna books one client at a time, a late cancellation leaves an empty chair that usually cannot be filled.

Repeated late cancellations may mean you are asked to prepay or leave a deposit for future appointments.

Late arrivals

If you are running late, please text as soon as you can. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your service may need to be shortened or rescheduled so the next client is not kept waiting. The full price of the scheduled service may still apply.

No-shows

A no-show is missing an appointment without any notice. Clients who do not show up may be asked to prepay for future appointments, and repeated no-shows may mean we are unable to book you again.

Deposits

Deanna may ask for a deposit for longer services, big color changes or new clients. Any deposit is applied to the cost of your service. Deposits are not refunded for late cancellations or no-shows unless Deanna agrees otherwise.

Payment

Payment is due when your service is finished. Ask Deanna which payment methods she currently accepts. Gratuity is never required and is always up to you.

Satisfaction and adjustments

If you are not happy with your color or cut, please let Deanna know within 7 days of your appointment. She will look at your hair and, where it makes sense, offer a complimentary adjustment.

  • Adjustments are for the original service only. A new look or a different service is a new appointment.
  • Changes caused by hair care at home, swimming, sun exposure, heat styling, box dye or another salon’s work are not covered.
  • Services are not refunded once they have been performed, because the time, skill and products have already been used.

Retail products

Unopened products may be returned within 14 days with proof of purchase for store credit. Opened products cannot be returned for health and safety reasons.

Children

Kids are welcome for haircuts. A parent or guardian must stay in the studio for the entire appointment for clients under 18. Color and chemical services for anyone under 18 require a parent or guardian’s consent.

The studio is a small working space with hot tools, sharp tools and chemicals. Children who are not being served must be supervised by their adult at all times.

Health and safety

Please reschedule if you are sick, have a contagious condition, or have any open cuts, sores, rashes or irritation on your scalp. Deanna may decline or stop a service if it is not safe to continue.

Please tell Deanna before your appointment about any allergies, sensitivities, skin or scalp conditions, medications, pregnancy, or recent chemical treatments, including box dye, relaxers, keratin treatments or henna. See the Service Disclaimer for more.

Guests and pets

The studio is small, so please keep extra guests to a minimum. Service animals are always welcome. Please ask before bringing any other animal.

Photos

Deanna sometimes takes photos of finished hair to show her work on this website and on social media. Photos are usually taken from behind or the side. She will ask before taking or sharing a photo, and you can always say no. If a photo of you has been shared and you would like it removed, text (602) 803-3496 and it will be taken down.

Right to refuse service

Deanna may decline or end a service for safety reasons, if a requested service is likely to damage your hair, or if a client is abusive or disruptive. She never refuses service for any reason prohibited by law.

Changes to these policies

These policies may be updated from time to time. The version posted on this page applies to appointments booked after the date it was updated.
