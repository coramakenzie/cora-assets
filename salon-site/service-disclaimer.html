Service Disclaimer

Last updated November 16, 2026

Hair services involve chemicals, heat and sharp tools. Please read this before your appointment. By booking and receiving a service from Bananas Hair, you agree to the following.

Results vary

Every head of hair is different. Results depend on your natural hair color, texture, condition, past treatments and home care. Photos on this website and on social media show past clients and are examples only. They are not a promise that your hair will look the same.

Some changes, such as going much lighter or removing old color, can take more than one appointment to do safely.

Appointment times

Times mentioned on this website, such as “about 90 minutes” for color and highlights, describe a typical appointment. Haircuts usually take less time. They are estimates and are not guaranteed.

Allergies and sensitivities

Hair color, lightener and other salon products can cause allergic reactions, including itching, redness, swelling or burning of the scalp, face or neck. In rare cases, reactions can be serious.

  • Tell Deanna about any known allergies or past reactions to hair dye, including reactions to temporary tattoos (black henna).
  • A patch test is recommended before your first color service and whenever you have had a reaction in the past. Ask Deanna to schedule one at least 48 hours before your appointment.
  • If you choose not to have a patch test, you accept the risk of a reaction.
  • If you notice any reaction during or after your service, tell Deanna right away and contact a doctor. Get emergency medical help if you have trouble breathing or swelling of the face, lips or throat.

Information you need to share

Please tell Deanna before your service about:

  • Any box dye, henna, relaxers, perms, keratin or smoothing treatments, or lightening done in the past two years, even if it has mostly grown out.
  • Scalp or skin conditions, recent sunburn, cuts or irritation.
  • Medications, medical conditions, or pregnancy that may affect your skin, scalp or hair.

Leaving out this information can cause unexpected color results, breakage or irritation. Bananas Hair is not responsible for results or reactions caused by information that was not shared.

Hair condition and damage

Lightening and chemical services change the structure of your hair. Hair that is already dry, damaged, or treated with other products is more likely to break. Deanna may recommend a different service, a strand test, or doing the work over more than one visit. If you choose to go ahead against her recommendation, you accept the risk to your hair.

Aftercare

How you care for your hair at home affects how long your color and cut last. Arizona sun, heat, hard water, chlorine and hot styling tools can fade color and cause brassiness. Follow the aftercare advice you are given.

Not medical advice

Deanna is a hairstylist, not a doctor. Any information about hair and scalp health on this website or during your appointment is general information, not medical advice. See a doctor or dermatologist about hair loss, scalp conditions or any health concern.

Assumption of risk and release
