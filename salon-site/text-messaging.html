Text Messaging Terms

Last updated November 16, 2026

Bananas Hair uses text messaging to book and manage appointments. These terms explain how texting works.

How texting works

When you text (602) 803-3496 or give us your number to book, you agree that Bananas Hair may text you about your appointments. This includes booking details, confirmations, reminders, schedule changes and replies to your questions.

These are personal, one-to-one messages about your appointments. We do not send automated marketing texts, and we never require you to agree to marketing messages to book a service.

Frequency and cost

How often you hear from us depends on how often you book, usually a few messages per appointment. Message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

Stopping texts

You can ask us to stop texting you at any time. Just reply “STOP” or tell us in a message. If you stop texts, we can still reach you by phone call to manage your appointments, if you would like.

Your information

We do not sell or share your phone number or messages with anyone for marketing. Please do not send sensitive information like credit card numbers by text. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages.
